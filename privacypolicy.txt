# DrinstBot Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** [DATE]

DrinstBot ("DrinstBot", "we", "us", or "our") provides tools and services that allow users to connect supported accounts and use features provided through DrinstBot.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we access, how we use it, how we store it, and how users can request deletion.

## 1. Information We Access

The information DrinstBot accesses depends on the platform and feature you use:

* **YouTube (through Google OAuth):** Your YouTube channel or account identifier and the YouTube API information needed to check whether your account is subscribed to a specified channel. We also receive OAuth access tokens, refresh tokens where provided, and token expiration information.
* **TikTok:** Your TikTok user identifier (such as an `open_id`), used to link your TikTok account with DrinstBot.
* **Instagram:** An Instagram-scoped ID (IGSID) for streamer accounts, used to identify and link the streamer account for the relevant feature. DrinstBot does not store Instagram OAuth tokens for this integration.
* **Other integrations, including Twitch:** Account or profile information returned by the platform that is needed for the specific feature. The exact information depends on the integration and permissions involved.

We do not request or store your password for any connected platform.

## 2. How We Use Connected Account Data

We use information obtained from a connected platform only to provide the features for which you authorized access.

Depending on the platform and feature, DrinstBot may use connected account information to:

* Identify the account you connected.
* Link a TikTok account using its platform user identifier, or identify a streamer account using its Instagram-scoped ID.
* Check whether a connected YouTube account is subscribed to a specified channel and whether its authorization remains valid.
* Perform later authorized YouTube API lookups without requiring you to authorize the same account every time.

YouTube OAuth tokens are stored so DrinstBot can maintain the authorized connection and recheck subscription status or connection validity later, using updated access when permitted and necessary.

We do not use connected account data for advertising, selling personal information, or unrelated profiling.

## 3. Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect stored account information and OAuth credentials from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

OAuth credentials are treated as sensitive information and are not intentionally made publicly accessible.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your connected account information.

We do not intentionally share your OAuth credentials or connected account information with unrelated third parties.

Information may be processed through the relevant third-party platform and its API as part of account authorization and feature functionality.

Each platform may have its own privacy policy, terms, and data practices that apply to your use of that platform.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain connected account information and OAuth credentials only for as long as they are reasonably necessary to provide the associated DrinstBot functionality or to maintain an authorized account connection.

If you disconnect an account or request deletion of your stored data, we will remove the associated information within a reasonable period, subject to legitimate technical, security, or legal requirements.

## 6. Disconnecting Your Account

You may revoke DrinstBot's access through the connected platform's account or authorization settings, where available.

You may also contact us to request deletion of the account information and OAuth credentials associated with your DrinstBot connection.

## 8. Third-Party Services

DrinstBot may use APIs and other integrations provided by third-party platforms, including Google and YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Twitch, or other services. The information accessed depends on the feature and the permissions you grant.

Your use of each third-party platform is subject to that platform's own terms, privacy policy, and other applicable policies.

Where a specific DrinstBot feature uses a particular platform, we may provide links to that platform's applicable policies.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or data practices change.

If we make material changes to how we access, use, or store connected account data, we will update this policy before using that data for the new purpose where required.

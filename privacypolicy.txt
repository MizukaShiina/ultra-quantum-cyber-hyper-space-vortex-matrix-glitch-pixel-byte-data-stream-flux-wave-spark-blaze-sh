# MizuBot Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** [DATE]

MizuBot ("MizuBot", "we", "us", or "our") provides tools and services that allow users to connect supported accounts and use features provided through MizuBot.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we access, how we use it, how we store it, and how users can request deletion.

## 1. Information We Access

When you choose to connect your YouTube account to MizuBot through Google OAuth, we may receive information associated with your authorized YouTube account, including:

* Your YouTube channel/account identifier.
* Information returned by the YouTube API that is necessary to provide the requested MizuBot feature.
* OAuth authentication tokens, including access tokens and, where provided, refresh tokens.
* Token expiration information.

We do not request or store your Google Account password.

## 2. How We Use YouTube Data

We use the information obtained through YouTube API Services only to provide the features for which you authorized access.

For example, MizuBot may use your YouTube account information to:

* Identify the YouTube account you connected.
* Check whether your account is subscribed to a specified YouTube channel.
* Perform later authorized YouTube API lookups without requiring you to authorize the same account every time.

OAuth tokens are stored so that MizuBot can maintain the authorized connection and obtain updated access when necessary.

We do not use your YouTube data for advertising, selling personal information, or unrelated profiling.

## 3. Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect stored account information and OAuth credentials from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

OAuth credentials are treated as sensitive information and are not intentionally made publicly accessible.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your YouTube information.

We do not intentionally share your OAuth credentials or YouTube account information with unrelated third parties.

Information may be processed through Google and the YouTube API as part of the account authorization and API functionality.

Our use of YouTube API Services is also subject to Google's and YouTube's applicable policies.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain YouTube account information and OAuth credentials only for as long as they are reasonably necessary to provide the associated MizuBot functionality or to maintain an authorized account connection.

If you disconnect your YouTube account or request deletion of your stored data, we will remove the associated information within a reasonable period, subject to legitimate technical, security, or legal requirements.

## 6. Disconnecting Your Account

You may revoke MizuBot's access to your Google account through your Google Account security settings.

You may also contact us to request deletion of the YouTube account information and OAuth credentials associated with your MizuBot connection.

Google account permissions can also be managed through Google's account security controls:

https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions

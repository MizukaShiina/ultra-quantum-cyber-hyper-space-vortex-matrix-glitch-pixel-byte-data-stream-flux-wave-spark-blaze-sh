# MizuBot Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** [DATE]

MizuBot ("MizuBot", "we", "us", or "our") provides tools and services that allow users to connect supported accounts and use features provided through MizuBot.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we access, how we use it, how we store it, and how users can request deletion.

## 1. Information We Access

When you choose to connect an account from a supported third-party platform to MizuBot, we may receive information associated with that authorized account, including:

* Your account or profile identifier on the connected platform.
* Information returned by the platform's API that is necessary to provide the requested MizuBot feature.
* OAuth authentication tokens, including access tokens and, where provided, refresh tokens.
* Token expiration information.

We do not request or store your password for any connected platform.

## 2. How We Use Connected Account Data

We use information obtained from a connected platform only to provide the features for which you authorized access.

Depending on the platform and feature, MizuBot may use connected account information to:

* Identify the account you connected.
* Check account status, eligibility, or activity required by a MizuBot feature.
* Perform later authorized API lookups without requiring you to authorize the same account every time.

OAuth tokens are stored so that MizuBot can maintain the authorized connection and obtain updated access when permitted and necessary.

We do not use connected account data for advertising, selling personal information, or unrelated profiling.

## 3. Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect stored account information and OAuth credentials from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

OAuth credentials are treated as sensitive information and are not intentionally made publicly accessible.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your connected account information.

We do not intentionally share your OAuth credentials or connected account information with unrelated third parties.

Information may be processed through the relevant third-party platform and its API as part of account authorization and feature functionality.

Each platform may have its own privacy policy, terms, and data practices that apply to your use of that platform.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain connected account information and OAuth credentials only for as long as they are reasonably necessary to provide the associated MizuBot functionality or to maintain an authorized account connection.

If you disconnect an account or request deletion of your stored data, we will remove the associated information within a reasonable period, subject to legitimate technical, security, or legal requirements.

## 6. Disconnecting Your Account

You may revoke MizuBot's access through the connected platform's account or authorization settings, where available.

You may also contact us to request deletion of the account information and OAuth credentials associated with your MizuBot connection.

## 7. Data Deletion Requests

To request deletion of information associated with a connected account, contact us at:

**Email:** [CONTACT EMAIL]

Please include enough information for us to identify the account or connection that should be removed.

We will review and process deletion requests in accordance with applicable law and the requirements of the relevant platform.

## 8. Third-Party Services

MizuBot may use OAuth, APIs, and other integrations provided by third-party platforms, which may include Google, YouTube, Discord, Twitch, or other services.

Your use of each third-party platform is subject to that platform's own terms, privacy policy, and other applicable policies.

Where a specific MizuBot feature uses a particular platform, we may provide links to that platform's applicable policies.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or data practices change.

If we make material changes to how we access, use, or store connected account data, we will update this policy before using that data for the new purpose where required.
